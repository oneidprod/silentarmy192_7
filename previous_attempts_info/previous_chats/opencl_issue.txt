## Summary

**Why commit 66498b8 sees 1 device but everything after sees 2:**

The key difference is the **linker flag** in the Makefile:

- **Commit 66498b8** (and earlier at 146be13): Uses `LDLIBS= -lcl -lrt`
  - This links directly against **Beignet's OpenCL library** 
  - Beignet only enumerates its own platform
  - **Result: 1 device detected** (Intel UHD Graphics via Beignet)

- **Commit 713bdda** and after: Uses `LDLIBS= -lOpenCL -lrt`
  - This links against the **generic OpenCL library** which uses the OpenCL ICD (Installable Client Driver) loader
  - The ICD loader discovers and enumerates **ALL available OpenCL platforms** on the system
  - **Result: 2 devices detected** (Beignet platform + Intel Gen OCL Driver platform)

**The fix:** Commit 713bdda ("Fix xi_offset_for_round formula for Equihash 192,7") not only fixed the xi_offset formula but also switched the linker back to `-lOpenCL`, which enabled discovery of all available OpenCL platforms through the ICD loader mechanism. This allows the solver to see both the Beignet driver and the native Intel OpenCL driver on your system.
